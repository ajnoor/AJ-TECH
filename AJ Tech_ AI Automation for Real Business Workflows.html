Instagram DM Automation

PROBLEM

Customers message businesses on Instagram expecting a fast reply. Most small teams can't keep up around the clock, and messages sit unanswered overnight.

SOLUTION

An AI agent wired directly into Instagram DMs through the Meta API and n8n. It reads intent, answers common questions in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, keeps context across the conversation, and escalates anything sensitive to a human.

THE HARD PART

Getting webhook verification, token auth and public exposure right, and stopping the bot from replying to its own messages, a real failure mode most tutorials skip over.

AI Order-Taking

PROBLEM

Taking orders over chat means manually copying names, addresses and product details into a spreadsheet: slow, and easy to get wrong.

SOLUTION

A conversational flow collects name, phone, address, product, variant and quantity, then writes it straight to Google Sheets, structured rather than a wall of chat text.

THE RULE THAT MATTERS

The AI never tells a customer their order is confirmed. Every order lands as Pending Human Confirmation until a team member reviews it.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What can AJ Tech actually automate?

Anything with a repeatable pattern: customer messages, order intake, lead capture, email handling. If it's a conversation or a process you're doing the same way every time, it's a candidate.

Can you automate my Instagram DMs?

Yes, that's one of the core builds. It connects through the Meta API and can handle English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, with escalation to a human when needed.

Can the AI take orders on its own?

It can collect and structure the order, but it never confirms one. Every order is marked pending until a person reviews it.

Do I need to review what the AI does?

For anything customer-facing or order-related, yes by design: a human checkpoint is built into the workflow, not bolted on afterward.

Can this connect to Google Sheets or tools I already use?

Usually. Sheets, Airtable, Supabase and most tools with an API or webhook can be wired in.

Can you automate my Gmail inbox?

Yes. Reading, classifying and extracting from incoming email, and triggering the right action.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the workflow. There's no fixed price list. Tell me what you need automated and I'll give you a straight answer.
